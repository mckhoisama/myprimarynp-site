Why Las Vegas is switching to telehealth primary care

A Medically reviewed by Dr. Ann, DNP, APRN, FNP-C · Las Vegas, NV

Las Vegas has one of the tightest primary-care markets in the country — average waits for a new-patient appointment stretch weeks, and many residents work nights and swing shifts that make daytime clinic hours nearly impossible. Telehealth flips that math.

What actually changes

With virtual primary care, the visit comes to you: same-week appointments, no drive down the 95, no waiting room, no time off work. Labs still happen in person — we send orders to a draw site near you — but everything else, from diagnosis to prescriptions to results review, happens on video.

What doesn't change

Good medicine. You still see a licensed Nevada provider — at myprimarynp, the same NP every time, who knows your history and remembers your last conversation. Every new patient starts with a real video visit, prescriptions are sent to a pharmacy you choose, and anything that needs hands-on care gets referred appropriately. Telehealth is a front door, not a shortcut.

Is it right for you?

  • Great for: everyday illness, blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes management, refills, weight loss, results review
  • Not for: emergencies, chest pain, injuries needing exams or imaging — that's urgent care or the ER
