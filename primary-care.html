Why an NP-led practice

Full prescriptive authority. More time with you.

01

Independent practice authority

Nevada is a full-practice-authority state — Ann diagnoses, treats, and prescribes under her own license. No referrals to wait on, no physician sign-off slowing down your care.

02

Doctorally prepared & board-certified

Ann is a DNP, board-certified as a family nurse practitioner (FNP-C), and carries independent malpractice coverage — the same accountability you'd expect from any clinic.

03

Continuity, on purpose

You're not re-explaining yourself to someone new every month. One NP who reads your chart before every visit and remembers you at the next one.
