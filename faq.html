FAQ

Everything people ask us. Answered honestly.

Do I have to get on video, or can I just fill out a form?
Every new patient starts with a live video visit — it's how good medicine works, and it's the safest way to prescribe in Nevada. After that first visit, quick questionnaire check-ins handle most refills.
Which weight-loss medications do you prescribe?
FDA-approved GLP-1 and dual-agonist medications only: Wegovy® (semaglutide, injection or daily pill), Zepbound® (tirzepatide), and Foundayo® (orforglipron, a once-daily pill). We never prescribe compounded copies.
How much does it really cost?
Weight loss membership is $129/month — visits, dose adjustments, messaging, labs coordination, and prior-auth help. Medication is billed separately: through insurance, or at the manufacturers' published cash prices (roughly $149–$449/month depending on the drug and dose).
Do you take insurance?
Your membership is self-pay, but we work with your insurance for the medication itself — including prior authorizations for Wegovy and Zepbound, and the Medicare $50/month Bridge program for those who qualify.
I don't live in Nevada. Can I still sign up?
Not yet — we're licensed in Nevada and see patients who are physically in Nevada at the time of their visit. We verify location at every appointment.
How do the medications actually ship to me?
After your video visit, your NP e-prescribes to a licensed pharmacy: your local pharmacy for insurance fills, or manufacturer-direct home delivery (LillyDirect®, NovoCare®) for cash pay. Cold-chain shipping is included for injectables.
What happens at the first video visit?
About 30 minutes: your history and goals, current medications, weight and vitals you report, and a plan — including baseline labs if needed. If a GLP-1 is appropriate, your prescription is sent the same day.
Do I need labs before starting?
Usually, yes — a baseline panel keeps you safe and gives us a starting line. We order labs at a draw site near you, and review results together in plain English.
Can I cancel my membership anytime?
Yes. Month-to-month, cancel in two clicks from your portal, no cancellation fees, no guilt trips.
What if I have side effects?
Message your NP directly — same day answers, dose adjustments when needed, and anti-nausea strategies that actually work. Managing side effects is a core part of the membership, not an upsell.
Is my information private?
Yes. Visits, messaging, and records live in a HIPAA-compliant platform, and we never sell or share your health information with advertisers.
